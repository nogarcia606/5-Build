Workshops

Hands-on learning in editing, cinematography, and storytelling.

Hands-On Sessions

These sessions are intended to be beginner-friendly and are led by local filmmakers.

  • Storyboarding and Shot Lists: Translating a short script into visual camera setups.
  • Lighting on a Micro-Budget: Shaping natural light and affordable LED kits for dramatic scenes.
  • Location Sound Basics: Capturing clean dialogue with boom mics and portable recorders.
  • Editing Pacing & Color: Cutting tight scenes and balancing color in post-production.

How to Prepare

  • Reserve Your Spot: Sign up ahead of time since hands-on sessions seats will fill up quickly.
  • Bring Your Own Gear: Cameras, smartphones, and laptops with editing software are welcome.
  • Bring an Idea: Bring a one-page scene or a rough concept to test during group exercises.
