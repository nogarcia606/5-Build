Film Screenings

Showcasing independent films from emerging creators.

Screening Night Schedules

Here is what to expect...

  1. Doors open and Mixer: Arrive early to grab seats and meet local filmmakers.
  2. Featured Film Block: Watch curated independent shorts or a feature presentation.
  3. Director Q&A: Stay after the credits for an open audience Q&A session.

Submit Your Own Short Film

This is where first-time or emerging filmmakers get to post their very own films!

Here are the requirements:

  • File formats accepted: MP4, MOV, DCP.
  • Short Films should be 15 to 20 minutes long, including credits.
