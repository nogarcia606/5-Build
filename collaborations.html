Collaboration

Connect with other creatives to bring ideas to life.

Who We Team Up With

We welcome both seasoned crew and newcomers building their first reels!

  • Cinematographers & Camera Operators: Framing shots, managing lenses, and shaping visual style on set.
  • Sound Mixers & Boom Operators: Capturing clean dialogue and ambient audio on location.
  • Editors & Colorists: Assembling rough cuts, pacing scenes, and grading final footage.

How We Can Start Collaborations

  1. Pitching Your Concept: Share a logline, treatment, or rough script with the collective.
  2. Match with Crew: Connect with writers, DPs, editors, or actors who fit your project's scope.
  3. Plan and Shoot: Lock in locations, schedules, and gear before rolling cameras.
  4. Credit and Screen Both: Ensure everyone receives proper screen credit and showcase the finished piece at a collective screening.
